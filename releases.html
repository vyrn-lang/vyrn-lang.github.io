Release process

A v* tag builds vyrn for Linux, macOS and Windows, checks it against the crate version, and publishes one SHA256SUMS beside the archives. A suffix makes it a pre-release.

  • The installers take the newest releasePin another with VYRN_VERSION.
  • Every example runs both waysByte for byte, on every push.
  • Verified, not stableThe route harness runs before a tag is cut.
